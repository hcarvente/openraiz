About Opportunities Archive

You belong here because of what you bring.

OpenRaíz connects young adults whose lived experience with systems — justice, immigration, foster care, housing instability, and others — has too often created barriers to opportunity, with organizations that recognize that experience as expertise, not a deficit.

Government agencies, nonprofits, philanthropy, and other mission-aligned employers list jobs, fellowships, internships, and volunteer roles here — opportunities that specifically welcome, prefer, or require the perspective you carry.

You don't have to disclose anything about your history to browse. Every listing tells you how it relates to lived experience; nothing here asks you to prove yours.
